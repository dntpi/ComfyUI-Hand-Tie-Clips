Two Ways to Join Shots (pin_mech)

    

      
        

          Mode
          Status & Best Use Case
          How It Works & Honest Tradeoffs
        

      
      
        

          motion_context
(Default — Built-In)		
          Stable
Continuous relays (~5–6 hops on Turbo, ~7–8 hops on non-Turbo with Refine) & multi-scene restart cuts		
          Built directly into Hand Tie Clips (no external custom node needed; credit to NikoDemon80's ComfyUI-H3-Motion-Context for the latent-slice + end-aligned audio timeline concept). Relays the previous hop's sampler AV latent into the next hop with in-latent head inpainting and distribution locking — and resets cleanly on restart cuts.
        

        

          hand_tie
(Single-Take Previs)		
          Public Experimental
Continuous one-location walking vlogs & tracking shots		
          Renders one low-res single-take bridge previs first and ties each full-res hop's opening and closing 0.9 s to those clean windows so hops never copy texture from each other.


          Previs Compiler Model (Recommended in ComfyUI/models/text_encoders/):

          Drop any qwen3vl_4b*.safetensors or qwen3vl_8b*.safetensors into models/text_encoders/ — e.g. qwen3vl_4b_fp8_scaled.safetensors (Comfy-Org), qwen3vl_4b (silveroxides/K2Q), or qwen3vl_8b_int8_convrot.safetensors (Comfy-Org). It loads at the start of the run to compile your beats into continuous bridge moments and unloads from VRAM before H3 starts.


          Caveat: Because every hop must meet its bridge window, beats that ask for a sudden location change, hard camera angle jump, or fast prop action across a join can cause the previs to cut or morph. It works very well when the camera and subject flow continuously in one setting, and may occasionally need a seed re-roll if a beat moves too far.		
        

      
    


    
Built-In Finishing (⚙ RUN Page)


    
1. Realism Boost + Audio Refine (Built for Simple Mode / 1–2 Hop Hero Clips)

    
    
      
  • Using HTC for standard single generations (ref2va / t2va): You don't need a multi-hop chain to use Hand Tie Clips — switch SCRIPT to Simple mode (chains = 1 in ⚙ RUN) and paste your standard 6-field MiniMax H3 ref2va / t2va prompt (subject_definitions:, summary:, detailed_description:, etc.) or plain prose. HTC passes structured H3 prompts straight through untouched while giving you the visual REFS rail, realism_boost, and upsample on a single clip!
    • 
      
  • realism_boost: on runs a 2-stage split schedule inside the sampler (at steps = 6: 2 base steps set composition, then 10 realism LoRA steps paint skin and lighting with no fresh noise).
    • 
      
  • Important Audio Note: Because stage 2 adds no noise, the audio stream only gets those first 2 noisy steps and will sound thin or under-converged on its own. When using realism_boost, turn on audio_refine: on (under the Soundtrack group, 4–5 steps) — it re-samples the soundtrack cleanly while keeping your finished video 100% frozen.
    • 
    


    
2. Refine Pass (hop_refine: full) — Honest Limitations

    
    
      
  • Runs a gentle low-sigma detail pass per hop (supports wiring a stock 30-step H3 DiT to refine_model while your main wire runs a Turbo model).
    • 
      
  • The honest truth on chain length: Refine holds back relay degradation and keeps joins clean, pushing continuous motion_context chains to 5–6 clips on Turbo before very visible texture changes appear, and 7–8 clips on non-Turbo. Under motion_context, plan to transition to a cut (anchor: "restart") every 4–5 clips or so to maintain the integrity of your textures (or use pin_mech: hand_tie for continuous single-take sequences).
    • 
    


    
3. Guided Tiled Upsampler (upsample: on) & Standalone Node

    
Enlarges the finished film to 1.5–2.0 MP in latent chunks and runs a 2-step tiled redraw. With the h3upscale x2 LoRA, the redraw is guided by your actual canvas frames at half the output size so it restores real detail instead of inventing texture. Toggling upsample on a cached chain skips hop generation and runs only the upscaler.


    

      Required Files for Upsampling:
      
    
        
  1. Latent Upscaler (`ComfyUI/models/latent_upscale_models/`):
    
            Download minimax_h3_latent_upscaler_3d_bf16.safetensors from LBH-123-AI/Minimax_h3_latent_Upscaler (or h3_upscaler_lms_v0.1.safetensors).
    2. 
        
  2. Guided Upscale LoRA (`ComfyUI/models/loras/`):
    
            Download h3upscale_v2_x2.safetensors from sandpies/ComfyUI-H3-Video-Upsampler (Hugging Face).
    3. 
        
  3. upsample_dit wire: When using h3upscale_v2_x2, wire a plain, unpatched H3 UNETLoader into upsample_dit (the LoRA was trained on the bare DiT without turbo/realism LoRAs underneath).
    4. 
      

      
Want to upscale any existing video outside a chain? Use the included H3 Video Upsampler (finished clip) node (workflows/HandTieClips_Upsample_Video.json) or the standalone repo: dntpi/ComfyUI-H3-Video-Upsampler.

Prompting Cheat-Sheet (The 5 Golden Rules)

    
MiniMax H3 samples at CFG 1.0 with no negative prompt — every word you write is added to the scene.

    

      
        

          ❌ Don't Write
          ✅ Write Instead
        

      
      
        

          "She stops talking." (Names talking → keeps her talking)
          "She smiles with her lips closed. The low hum of the refrigerator."
        

        

          "No background music, silent room." (Silence = model invents speech)
          Always name a specific room tone: "Soft rain on the windowpane, distant traffic hum."
        

        

          Re-describing hair, face, and clothes on Shots 2, 3, 4
          Describe only the new action on Shots 2+ — the reference rail and join pin carry her look.
        

        

          Ending Shot 2 mid-sentence when Shot 3 should be quiet
          Finish the action/sentence at the end of Shot 2 so Shot 3 inherits a settled state.
        

        

          Leaving the final shot's tail on ongoing
          Set the last shot's tail to settle or hold so the film lands cleanly.
